The movement

From a party to a community

Prince 365 grew into a Clubhouse community, and the conversation went on the road: fan trips Rodney curated to Minneapolis and Los Angeles, including the Club Prince 365 Minneapolis Takeover, October 21st–24th 2021, and a movie theater outing at Chanhassen Cinema with PRINCE 365 up on the marquee. Online, the group pulled off a legendary 18-hour Prince Clubhouse event, remembered in December 2023 with Purple All Night Part Deux.

About the founder

Rodney Fitzgerald aka Fitz, The C.E.O.

Purple Pundit | Creator of Prince 365 | Host of Club Prince 365 Clubhouse Podcast | Retro Gamer | Child of Hip Hop & House | Grew up at a HBCU | Found Heaven 4 the 2nd time | Made in K A Ψ | CowboyNation 4 Life

Rodney Fitzgerald is the CEO and creator of Prince 365 LLC. Prince 365 is a social group dedicated to celebrating the legacy of Prince, hosting events and discussions around his music and influence.

He organizes events and manages brand marketing for music fan clubs, has facilitated excursions in cities like Minneapolis and Los Angeles, and hosts Q&A sessions with notable figures. He is the host of the Club Prince 365 Clubhouse podcast, “The Premiere Prince Clubhouse Group,” and also creates media content related to Prince.
